About Us

Built for events that need real engagement

Lumina Vote is a voting and ticketing platform built by Campus Plug for organizers who need a fast, transparent way to run competitions and sell tickets online.

Who we are

Lumina Vote is a product of Campus Plug, a Ghana-based company that also runs campusplug.app, a marketplace connecting students across Ghana's universities, technical universities, and training colleges. We built Lumina Vote to bring that same local, no-nonsense approach to event voting and ticketing — pageants, talent shows, award nights, and campus events that need a fair, trackable way to collect votes or sell tickets.

Every vote and every ticket sale on Lumina Vote is recorded and reflected in real time, so organizers and voters can both see exactly where things stand.

What we do

Voting Competitions Organizers create events, add contestants, and votes get tallied live.
Ticketed Events Sell tickets online with QR codes and check-in at the door.
Live Results Anyone can follow a leaderboard as it updates in real time.

Our approach

We keep things local, honest, and simple — the same principles behind everything Campus Plug builds. That means clear pricing, no hidden fees, and support you can actually reach when something goes wrong.
